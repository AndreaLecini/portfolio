Belief State

Master's thesis — LLM-driven characters

Researcher & Developer

Belief State gives non-player characters a persistent, structured memory of what they know. Instead of relying on prompting alone, each character's knowledge is held in a graph that updates as the story unfolds, and every line the language model generates is constrained to that state.

The result is characters who remember what they have been told, stay consistent across long conversations, and stop inventing events that never happened. Against a prompting-only baseline, the approach improved narrative coherence and believability by 28%.

A real-time environment built to see how far photorealism can be pushed while staying fully interactive. Geometry runs on Nanite and lighting on Lumen, so the scene keeps its full detail and dynamic global illumination without baking.

Parts of the environment were reconstructed from real-world captures through photogrammetry and Gaussian splatting, then integrated with authored assets. Characters were built with MetaHuman and animated for a set of cinematic sequences, with Niagara driving the atmospheric effects.
